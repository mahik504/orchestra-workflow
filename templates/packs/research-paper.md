# Pack — research paper

Load when the job is a methods paper, arXiv note, or academic write-up. Not a reading UI.

- Graph: `research-paper` (not `academic-reader` unless they asked for a viewer).
- Skill: `orchestra-docs`.
- No Design Lab unless they asked for a shown figure UI.
- Cite real papers. Do not invent metrics.

Skip Stitch, GetLayers, and motion kits.
