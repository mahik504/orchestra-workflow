# Research paper

## Order

1. Title, authors, and the claim in one paragraph.
2. What is already known, with citations.
3. What this paper did.
4. Results that are already in the draft or the data files.
5. What the result does not show.
6. References.

## Citations

- Keep the authors and the numbers already in the draft.
- Check each DOI on CrossRef before it stays in the paper. Remove a DOI that does not resolve.
- Do not invent a citation, a metric, or a quotation.
- Figures come from the paper's data, or from PaperBanana when the user names it.
- A missing figure is better than a drawn one.

Humanizer may pass the prose. It may not change results.

The draft leads with the result. Collaborators are named. A citation that you did not open does not stay in the bibliography.
