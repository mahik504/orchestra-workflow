# Research paper

| Step | Do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Keep the authors and the numbers already in the draft |
| 2 | Check each DOI against CrossRef before you cite it |
| 3 | Figures come from the paper's data or from PaperBanana when the user names it |
| 4 | Humanizer may pass the prose. It may not change results |

A wrong DOI is a failure. A missing figure is better than a fake one.
